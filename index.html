KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Three threats worth knowing

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. It is also about recognising warning signs and taking a moment to check.

✉

THREAT 01

Phishing

Fraudulent emails, texts or websites try to trick you into revealing information or clicking a harmful link.

  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Be cautious with urgent requests.
  • Visit sites using a trusted address.
🔑

THREAT 02

Weak or reused passwords

If one password is exposed, reusing it can put several of your accounts at risk.

  • Use long, unique passwords.
  • Consider a reputable password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
⬇

THREAT 03

Unsafe downloads

Unexpected files or untrusted software can contain malware or collect information without permission.

  • Download from trusted sources.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments.
